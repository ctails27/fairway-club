Fairway Club (“the app”, “we”) is a golf scorekeeping app for tee times, pairings, standings and side games among friends. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

1. The short version

  • You can use the app without an account. Then everything stays on your phone (in your browser’s storage) and is never sent to us.
  • If you sign in, your data is stored in Google Firebase so it can sync between your devices and with groups you join.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data at any time from Account → Delete account in the app.
  • The app never holds, moves or processes money.

2. Information the app handles

  • Account information (only if you sign in): your name, email address and sign-in method (email/password or Google). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Golf information you enter: player names, course handicaps and optional handicap IDs, optional phone numbers or emails you add for players, scores and rounds, tee times, sign-ups, pairings, custom courses, and side-game settings and results.
  • Group information: the groups you create or join, their members’ names and course handicaps, invite codes, and live rounds shared with the group.
  • Technical information: the app stores data in your browser’s local storage and offline cache so it works without signal. Google’s services may log technical data (such as IP address, device and browser type) to operate and secure the service.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, payment information or government ID, and the app has no analytics or advertising trackers [confirm before publishing].

If you add another person as a player (for example their name and handicap), please make sure they are comfortable with that.

3. How the information is used

  • To keep score, calculate side-game results and standings, and show them to you and your group.
  • To sync your data across your devices and with groups you choose to share with.
  • To secure accounts, prevent abuse and fix problems.

4. Who can see it

  • Just you: data in your personal space.
  • Your group: everything you sync to a group is visible to, and editable by, all of its members.
  • Service providers: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and hosting) processes data on our behalf as a data processor, under Google’s terms and privacy policy (linked below). Data may be stored and processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Firebase privacy and security information

No sale of data. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

  • Cloud data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). This deletes your sign-in, your profile and your personal cloud data, and removes you from groups. If you own a group you can hand it to another member or delete it. Rounds that a group saved stay with that group unless the group is deleted.
  • Data stored only on your phone can be removed with Clear all data in the app or by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • Backup copies held by Google may take additional time to expire [confirm retention period].
  • You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information by writing to [contact email].

7. Age requirements

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their information. Tracking side-game amounts in dollars is for adults 18 and older only; younger users may use scorekeeping and points mode. [Attorney: confirm age thresholds.]

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and cloud access is restricted by security rules so only you (or members of your group) can read it. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password.

9. Money and side games

Fairway Club tracks side games only and never holds or moves money. It doesn’t collect payment details or process payments of any kind.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
